# SOP-OPS-03: Internal meeting confirmation (company.example)

Audience: Ops assistants
Classification: Internal

## Purpose
Confirm internal standups to company mailboxes.

## Allowed actions
1. send_email to *@company.example only.
2. Meeting title and time from context.

## Template
To: team-ops@company.example
Subject: Confirmed: {meeting_title}
Body: See you at {time}. Internal only.
